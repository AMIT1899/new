FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can I pay online?

No. The current static build supports Cash on Delivery only. UPI and cards are intentionally shown as unavailable.

Why is my order missing on another device?

Orders are stored in LocalStorage on the browser where you placed them. Cross-device history requires a real backend and authentication.

How do I add products?

Edit js/products.js and upload corresponding images under assets/images/products/.
