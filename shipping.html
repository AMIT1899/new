Shipping Policy

Shipping logic used by the NORTHROW static store.

Shipping charges

Orders at or above ₹1,499 after discounts ship free. Lower-value orders include a ₹99 shipping charge.

Delivery estimate

The front-end pincode checker displays an estimated 3–7 business day window for structurally valid Indian PIN codes. It is not connected to a courier API.
